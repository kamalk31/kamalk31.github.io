I am a PhD scholar and Junior Research Fellow (JRF) in Statistics at the
Institute of Science, Banaras Hindu University (BHU), Varanasi. I began my PhD
in February 2026, funded by the UGC-JRF fellowship. My work is in statistical
modelling, reliability and survival analysis.

## Research interests

- Competing risks and multistate models
- Bayesian survival analysis
- Variational inference
- Reliability and statistical inference
- Epidemiological methods

## Background

I completed my M.Sc. in Statistics at BHU (September 2023 – June 2025) and my
B.Sc. in Mathematics and Statistics at IGNTU Amarkantak (October 2020 – June
2023). I qualified the UGC NET-JRF in December 2025 with a percentile of 99.71.

## Earlier work

My M.Sc. thesis studied heteroscedasticity and multicollinearity in regression
models. Using real data on automobile mileage and U.S. trade indicators, I
diagnosed these problems with the Breusch-Pagan test, White's test and
correlation matrices. I then compared ordinary least squares with weighted
least squares, ridge regression and principal component regression in R and
Excel, judging the improvement by adjusted R², RMSE and residual standard error.
