I am a PhD scholar and Junior Research Fellow (JRF) in Statistics at Banaras
Hindu University (BHU), Varanasi. I began my PhD in February 2026, funded by
the UGC-JRF fellowship. My work is in statistical modelling, reliability and
survival analysis.

## Research interests

- Competing risks and multistate models
- Bayesian survival analysis
- Variational inference
- Reliability and statistical inference

## Background

I completed my M.Sc. in Statistics at BHU (2023–2025) and my B.Sc. in
Mathematics and Statistics at IGNTU Amarkantak (2020–2023). I qualified the
UGC NET-JRF in December 2025 with a percentile of 99.71.

## Earlier work

My M.Sc. thesis studied heteroscedasticity and multicollinearity in regression
models. Using real datasets, I diagnosed these problems with the Breusch-Pagan
and White tests and compared remedies such as weighted least squares, ridge
regression and principal component regression, implemented in R.

## Tools

Python (Pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn, Matplotlib), R, and Excel.
