I am a PhD scholar and Junior Research Fellow (JRF) in Statistics at the
Institute of Science, Banaras Hindu University (BHU), Varanasi. 
I began my PhD in February 2026, funded by the UGC-JRF fellowship.

## Research interests

- Competing risks and multistate models
- Bayesian survival analysis
- Variational inference
- Reliability and statistical inference

## Background

I completed my M.Sc. in Statistics at BHU ( 2023 – 2025) and my
B.Sc. in Mathematics and Statistics at IGNTU Amarkantak ( 2020 – 2023). 
I qualified the UGC NET-JRF in December 2025 with a percentile of 99.71.
